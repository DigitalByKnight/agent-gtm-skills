The GTM blind spot

Ask your AI agent to price a product. You get a blog post about value-based pricing from 2021. Ask it to build a cold outreach sequence. You get a 7-email template that belongs in spam.

These models are capable. They just lack structured GTM knowledge to work from. Without real frameworks, current benchmarks, and decision matrices, every agent defaults to the same surface-level playbook.

The GTM Stack fixes that. 18 skill files, each 300-900 lines of dense, opinionated playbooks covering one GTM function. Drop them into your agent and the output changes immediately.

Three steps to better GTM

01

Install

One command drops the skill files into your agent's context. Works with any tool that reads .md files.

02

Ask

Describe what you need in plain language. "Build a cold outreach sequence" or "How should I price my AI product?" The right skills activate automatically.

03

Execute

Your agent works from dense playbooks with real numbers instead of generic training data. The output is specific, current, and actionable.

Start with a workflow

Pick the goal closest to what you're building. Skills combine automatically.
